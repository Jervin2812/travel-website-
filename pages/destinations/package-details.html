Expedition Overview

Immerse yourself in authentic Indonesian tradition, breathtaking landscapes, and tropical serenity. This curated journey combines private luxury villa accommodations with exclusive access to UNESCO cultural heritage sites, private boat excursions to Nusa Penida, and personalized holistic wellness treatments.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Fully Guided • Private Transit

Arrive at the international airport where our dedicated airport concierge and private chauffeur greet you. Settle into your private villa in Ubud followed by a chef-prepared welcome dinner featuring organic regional ingredients and live traditional gamelan music.

Included Meals: Dinner • Stay: Royal Pita Maha Sanctuary

Awaken to guided jungle yoga followed by an exclusive morning walk through the sacred Monkey Forest. In the afternoon, visit an organic spice farm and learn authentic cooking secrets from a master Balinese chef.

Included Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Experience an early morning visit to the UNESCO emerald rice terraces before crowds arrive. Participate in a traditional water blessing ritual led by a Hindu priest at Tirta Empul holy spring.

Included Meals: Breakfast, Refreshments

Board a private 45ft sailing catamaran to explore the dramatic limestone cliffs of Nusa Penida, snorkel with manta rays at Manta Point, and relax on pristine private white sand beaches.

Included Meals: Breakfast, Gourmet Seafood Lunch

Enjoy a day of bespoke relaxation with a 2-hour volcanic hot stone massage, followed by sunset cocktails overlooking the Indian Ocean at Uluwatu Temple and an unforgettable private farewell gala on Jimbaran Beach.

Included Meals: Breakfast, Farewell Gala Dinner
