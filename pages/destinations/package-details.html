Expedition Overview

Immerse yourself in authentic Indonesian tradition and tropical splendor. This curated journey combines private luxury villa accommodations with exclusive access to UNESCO cultural heritage sites, private boat excursions to Nusa Penida, and personalized holistic wellness treatments.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive at Denpasar International Airport where our private concierge greets you. Check in to your private jungle villa in Ubud followed by a chef-curated traditional welcome dinner.

Experience early morning mist over emerald terraces before visiting centuries-old artisan workshops and receiving a private blessing at Tirta Empul holy water temple.

Charter a private speedboat to the rugged cliffs of Kelingking T-Rex Beach, followed by guided snorkeling in crystal turquoise waters with wild manta rays.

Transfer to an oceanfront resort in southern Bali. Enjoy leisure spa treatments, seafood barbecues in Jimbaran Bay, and seamless chauffeur return to the airport.
